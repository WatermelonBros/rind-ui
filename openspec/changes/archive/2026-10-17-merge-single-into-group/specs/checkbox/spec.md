## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Anatomy

**Reason**: Checkbox is no longer a component of its own: a lone `CheckboxGroup.Item` (with `CheckboxGroup.ItemIndicator`) is the single control (user decision: no single+group duplicates).

**Migration**: the requirement moves to the `checkbox-group` capability as "Item Anatomy"; `Checkbox.Root` becomes `CheckboxGroup.Item` and `Checkbox.Indicator` becomes `CheckboxGroup.ItemIndicator`.
