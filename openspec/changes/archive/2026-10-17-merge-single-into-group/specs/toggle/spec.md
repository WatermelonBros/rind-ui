## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Anatomy

**Reason**: Toggle is no longer a component of its own: a lone `ToggleGroup.Item` (with `ToggleGroup.ItemIndicator`) is the single control (user decision: no single+group duplicates).

**Migration**: the requirement moves to the `toggle-group` capability as "Item Anatomy"; `Toggle.Root` becomes `ToggleGroup.Item` and `Toggle.Indicator` becomes `ToggleGroup.ItemIndicator`.
