Agent Presence: the theme draws the Visual as a dark glass orb with a ribbon of coloured light, its shape and motion set by the state (a thin dim line when idle, a dot that stretches while connecting, a cool ribbon rising with the voice while listening, a turning ribbon while thinking, a wide warm ribbon that swells and glows while speaking, a flat line on error). New optional token `--rind-agent-presence-color`.
