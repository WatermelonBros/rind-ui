Add Calendar: a month grid on `@internationalized/date` (calendar systems via `createCalendar`, locale week starts, `today` from an injected clock). Single and multiple selection, a focused date with roving focus and APG keys (days, weeks, Home/End, months, years with Shift), several visible months with `pageBehavior`, min/max (disabled) and unavailable dates, read-only and disabled, triggers that stay focusable at the bounds, polite range announcements from outside the grid. The `getCalendar` driver, `fixedDateClock`, and `calendar.css`. The keyboard layer gains the `grid` orientation.
