Add Listbox: single, multiple and no selection, toggle and replace behaviours (selection follows focus, mod to move only), actions, sections, descriptions, empty and load-more, horizontal and RTL, all built on the collection pieces. The listbox parts are reusable under another scope (`listboxParts`, `createListboxParts`) for Select and Combobox. The `getListbox` driver, `listboxTraits`, and `listbox.css`.
