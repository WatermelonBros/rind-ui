Timeline: an ordered list of events in reading order, with statuses (completed, current, pending, upcoming) per Item or derived from `current`, spoken in each title and `aria-current="step"` on the current event; `align` (start, end, alternate) and `orientation` as data; `Timeline.Time` with a machine-readable `datetime`. Testing: `getTimeline`. Theme: `timeline.css` (statuses by shape and colour).
